01 The problem

Ready-made software is built for the average business.

Nobody runs an average business. Your hours, your team, your customers and your way of doing things are your own.

  1. 01

    You pay for features you never open.

    Most plans bundle dozens of modules. Your team uses a handful and clicks past the rest every day.

  2. 02

    Your team works around the app.

    Extra steps, workarounds and a spreadsheet on the side, because the software was designed for someone else’s process.

  3. 03

    Your numbers live in five places.

    Sales in one app, stock in another, tasks in a WhatsApp group. Nobody sees the whole picture until someone puts it together by hand.

02 What we build

Two kinds of tools. Both made around you.

01

Internal team tools

Checklists, task lists, approvals and staff apps that follow your exact process. Your team opens one screen and knows what to do next.

  • Opening and closing checklists
  • Stock counts and handovers
  • Requests and approvals
02

Dashboards and reports

Your key numbers on one page, built around the questions you ask every week, from the files and systems you already use.

  • Sales by branch or by day
  • Stock levels and reorders
  • Weekly reports for owners

03 How we work

We learn how you work first. Then we build.

  1. Step 1

    Talk

    A short call on WhatsApp or video about how your business runs today and where time gets lost.

  2. Step 2

    Plan

    We write down what the tool will do and show you the screens before any code is written.

  3. Step 3

    Build

    We build in small parts. You try each one as it is ready and tell us what to change.

  4. Step 4

    Support

    We train your team, fix problems and keep improving the tool after launch.

05 Questions

Before we talk.

How much does a custom tool cost?

It depends on what the tool needs to do. After a first conversation about how your business works, we send you a quote before any work starts.

Do we have to change how our team works?

No. That is the point. We start from how your team works today and build the tool around it.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. We build in English and Arabic, including right-to-left screens.

Can we see something you have built?

Yes. RevenueRoll, FoodLoop and KitchenOS are our own tools for restaurants. They are live and free to try.

Which businesses do you work with?

Businesses of any type in the UAE and GCC. Most of our own tools so far are for restaurants and cafes.
