06 Questions

Before you upload anything.

Is RevenueRoll free?

Yes. The current dashboard costs nothing to use. When direct POS connections and paid features arrive, we will tell you the price before anything changes.

Can I use it for more than one branch?

Yes. Each branch manager sees only their branch. Owners see every branch, whether you run one site in Sharjah or six across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Which POS systems does it work with?

Any POS that exports sales to Excel or CSV. Direct connections to Foodics, Revel and Square are planned, so you will not need to export by hand later.

Does it track Talabat, Deliveroo and Noon Food?

Yes, as long as the orders are in your POS export. Direct connections to the delivery apps are planned.

What about UAE VAT?

Sales are in AED today. VAT-aware supplier costs and FTA-ready food cost records come with Uniment Inventory, which is in development.

Who can see my restaurant’s data?

Only people you add. Each restaurant’s data is kept separate from every other customer, and we do not sell it.
